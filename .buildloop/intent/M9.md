# Intent: M9

**Asked (2026-11-19):** after M8, "whats next"; the owner said "go" and ruled out iCloud sync,
diagnostics, iPad and TestFlight ("do not do 5 and 6"). Item 1 of the proposed list was
protecting the decisions file.

**Problem:** any failure to load decisions.json produced an empty book, and the next save wrote
it over the file: every keep, queue, streak and history entry gone. A corrupt file, or Pictr
woken by Siri or Shortcuts before the first unlock after a restart, was enough. Failed writes
were silent.

**Wanted:** decisions are never lost to a load or write failure, and the owner is told when
something is wrong. No change to the file format.
