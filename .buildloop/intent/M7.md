# Intent: M7, Pictr 0.2: a feature-rich release

**id:** M7   **originator:** Zac   **date:** 2026-11-19

## The ask (originator's words)
"pick back up where we left off and go wild and make this super feature rich and commit
and merge and ship"

## Reading
Where we left off: 0.1.1 (build 2) shipped; the last session put a swipe deck in every
Cleanup suggestion. The open queue item is T2.4 (iCloud sync, share from deck, iPad,
opt-in diagnostics). "Go wild" hands the scope to the agent: add as much real,
on-brand capability as one verified release can carry.

## Chosen scope
1. Deck: swipe up to favorite (with a heart button and undo), share the original from the
   deck and the viewer, a Shuffle deck of random unreviewed photos from any year, and an
   On This Day range of one day or the whole week.
2. Cleanup: an exact Duplicates suggestion that needs no analysis, a Library insights page
   (what takes space, by kind and year, largest items, on this iPhone vs iCloud), and a
   CSV export of cleanup history.
3. Siri and Shortcuts: App Shortcuts that open Today, Shuffle, Cleanup and Ready to
   Delete, and one that answers how many photos wait in Ready to Delete.

## Constraints (inherited, unchanged)
- No networking code, no analytics, on-device only. iPhone only.
- Never delete without Apple's confirmation; never auto-select suggestions (a group with a
  protected keeper may preselect its extras, as Similar shots already does).
- Honest space copy: never "freed". No em dashes.
- Swift 6 strict concurrency, warnings as errors, no third-party dependencies.

## Deliberately out of scope (and why)
- iCloud sync of progress: CloudKit entitlements and an account dependency; infra, and it
  contradicts "No account". Needs the originator.
- iPad: CLAUDE.md fixes iPhone only.
- Opt-in diagnostics: contradicts "no analytics".
- Home Screen widget: needs a new extension bundle ID and an App Group registered on the
  developer account; outward-facing, so it waits for a yes.
- App Store / TestFlight upload: production, behind the deploy gate.

## Open
- Sharing must fetch an iCloud original when the photo is not on the iPhone. That is a new,
  user-initiated iCloud fetch path; CLAUDE.md and README must say so.
